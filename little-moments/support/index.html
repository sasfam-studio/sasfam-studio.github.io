Help and Support

Something not working, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email colourlittlemoments@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. It helps to mention your phone model and which page you were colouring.

How do I unlock more pictures?

Finish a page by hand and the next picture unlocks for free. You also get free picks every month, and you can spend the coins you earn by colouring on any page you like.

What does "Finish for me" do?

Once a page is at least half coloured, "Finish for me" fills in the rest. Pages finished this way don't earn coins or unlock new pictures, so colour by hand when you want rewards.

How do I start a page again?

Open the page and tap "Start again" at the top. This clears your colours so you can begin fresh.

How do I save or share my picture?

Finished pages appear in your finished pages. From there you can save them to your photos, share them, or make a drawing book.

Will I lose my pictures if I delete the app?

Yes. Everything is kept only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes your pages and coins too. Save your favourite pages to your photos first.
