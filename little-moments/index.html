Colour Your Little Moments

A cosy colouring book to relax.

Colour gentle everyday scenes, one little moment at a time: a girl and her puppy, a rainy-day puddle jump, a coffee date, a quiet railway halt and more.

What you can do

  • Fill areas with a tap, or paint freely with soft brushes, pencils and watercolour.
  • Pinch to zoom in on the tiny details.
  • Peek at a coloured example when you need ideas.
  • Finish a page to unlock the next picture, and earn coins as you colour.
  • Save your pages, or put them together into your own little colouring book.

Calm by design

  • No login and no ads.
  • Works offline.
  • Your pictures stay on your phone.
