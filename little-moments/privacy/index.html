Privacy Policy

Colour Your Little Moments · Sasfam Studio
Last updated: 28 October 2026

Colour Your Little Moments ("the app") is made by Sasfam Studio. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we don't collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or login. It does not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts, and it does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else.

Your pictures and progress

Your coloured pages, drawing books, coins and unlocked pages are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, this information is deleted from your device too.

Photos permission

If you choose to save a page or drawing book to your photos, the app asks for permission to add pictures to your photo library. The app uses this only to save the pictures you choose. It does not read or upload your other photos.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's own sharing screen opens and you choose where the picture goes. We don't see or keep what you share.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app shows no ads. It contains no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The app currently has no real-money purchases. If optional purchases are added in the future, payments will be handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We will never see your card or payment details. This policy will be updated before any such change.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email colourlittlemoments@gmail.com.
